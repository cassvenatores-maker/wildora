1. Executive Summary

Wildora is an "Idle to Earn" and collection RPG focused on an immersive experience on Telegram. The player accumulates energy over time, which is converted into distance traveled in-game. The further the player travels, the rarer and more valuable the chests, monsters, and rewards become.

The absolute differentiator of Wildora is the elimination of boredom: the player progresses while living their life, returning to the app only to make strategic decisions (open chests, hatch eggs, merge monsters, and invest in the TON network).

2. The Economic Solution

Web3 games often suffer from token hyperinflation due to the ease of generating coins without a corresponding burning mechanism (sinks). Wildora solves this with a closed ecosystem highly incentivized for reinvestment:

  • Monsters as NFTs (Future): Monsters have in-game value (generate multipliers) and speculative value.
  • Real spending in TON: Permanent upgrades (like expanding the radar or speeding up incubators) require direct payment in TON or USDT, generating real revenue for the project's Liquidity Pool.

3. Tokenomics: The WILA Token

4. Burn Architecture (Sinks) and Sustainability

The health of a Web3 game lies in its ability to make the player spend the currency. Implemented mechanisms:

  • Merge Roulette: Merging 5 common monsters has a 20% failure rate, destroying all of them. This acts as a deflationary sink for NFTs/Tokens.
  • Short-Term Consumables: Items like "Radar Boost" cost real money or tokens, last a short time, and create an addictive loop of constant spending to stay ahead in Leagues.

HUNTER'S MANUAL: OFFICIAL SURVIVAL GUIDE

1. The Radar and the Backpack Rule

The core of the game. While you live your life, the Radar collects distance over time. Every 1,000m, you earn 1 reward chest.

Attention: The Radar is a "backpack" with a maximum limit (e.g., 4500m at level 1). If the limit is reached, you stop generating chests. Enter the game and press "Claim" to empty the radar and continue earning.

2. Chest Mathematics

Opened a chest? The gods of luck roll the dice:

  • 40% chance of finding Food (Essential in-game currency to level up).
  • 60% chance of finding a Mysterious Egg.

3. The Incubator and the "Egg Crusher"

You only have ONE incubator by default. Eggs take a long time to hatch (Common eggs take hours, Epics can take days).

If your egg inventory fills up, new chests will lose the eggs (they are discarded). The solution? Buy an Acceleration Oven in the Shop (with real money/TON) and decrease hatching time by 15x!

4. The Path to Level 10

Monsters are born weak (Level 1). You must feed them (Spending Food). Each level they gain drastically increases your Mining Power for the future Airdrop.

The current maximum level is 10. You have to decide: Focus all your food on your favorite Epic monster, or level up several common ones for "Merging"?

5. The Art of Merging

Duplicate monsters aren't trash, they are your greatest weapon. When you gather 5 identical monsters that are at the maximum level (10), you can use the Fusion Chamber.

Fusion transforms 5 "Common" monsters into 1 "Rare" from the same biome. But beware: Fusion has a failure rate to burn inflation. Be strategic.
